Crossroads Infrastructure Inc.

North Carolina Roadway Contractor

Company Overview

Crossroads Infrastructure Inc. operates as a heavy civil engineering contractor serving Greensboro, North Carolina, and surrounding areas. We manage comprehensive concrete construction, roadway realignments, and storm drainage networks for NCDOT and various municipalities.

Core Specializations

Concrete Construction

Precision installation of concrete curb & gutter lines, standard pedestrian sidewalks, commercial driveways, traffic islands, and regulatory ADA-compliant curb ramps.

Grading & Excavation

Heavy site preparation management including structural earthwork grading, technical right-of-way clearing, grubbing sequences, and subgrade stabilization.

Storm Water Systems

Installation of high-capacity masonry drainage catch basins, surface run-off structures, and underground storm drainage pipeline configurations.

Retaining Walls

Comprehensive layout installation and modular reconstruction parameters for Mechanically Stabilized Earth (MSE), Cast-in-Place, and Segmental Gravity Retaining Walls.

Recent Site Projects

Davie St Project Operations

Davie Street Streetscape

A targeted municipal infrastructure upgrade in Greensboro, North Carolina, focused on precision concrete construction, comprehensive storm, water, and sewer utility construction, and the integration of various decorative site furnishings.

High Point Site Operations

City of High Point Resurfacing

A major multi-street roadway improvement deployment involving extensive concrete curb and gutter construction alongside precision utility adjustments, specializing in the raising, lowering, and shifting of active municipal manholes and water valve boxes.
